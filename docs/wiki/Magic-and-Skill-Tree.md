# Magic and Skill Tree

Magic combines mana-powered equipment, school mastery, and connected research skills. The **MP** bar shares the player's HP status panel.

## Casting

Assign a magical item to the hotbar, select it, aim, and left-click. Casting requires enough mana, the relevant unlocks, and completion of its recovery time. Magical equipment cannot be used to mine blocks.

Mana regenerates after a casting delay. Mana Tonics restore mana. As school mastery increases, maximum mana can increase as well.

## Schools

| School | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Destruction | Elemental damage, burning, slowing, piercing, and lightning |
| Restoration | Healing and regeneration |
| Warding | Damage-absorbing barriers |
| Illumination | Exploration light and spell-weaving research |
| Necromancy | Drain, blight, and raising fallen allies |

School mastery has five ranks, with thresholds at 0, 30, 90, 180, and 300 mastery points. Effective use and supported crafting activities develop the relevant school.

## Research

Press **K** to open the magic skill and spell guide. New characters begin with three research points. Increasing school ranks provides additional points.

Skills can require a point, a minimum school rank, and connected prerequisite skills. Buying a node unlocks the described capability; owning an item alone does not bypass research requirements.

Illumination includes **Dual weaving**, **Expanding sigils**, and **Linked sigils**, which unlock combined effects and additional delivery forms for custom spells.

## Existing magical equipment

- **Ember Wand:** Fire damage with a burning effect.
- **Frost Wand:** Ice damage with a slowing effect.
- **Storm Staff:** Lightning that chains between visible enemies.
- **Crystal Staff:** A piercing crystal projectile.
- **Mending Tome:** Direct healing when injured.
- **Ward Book / Greater Ward Book:** Temporary protective barriers.
- **Lumen Tome:** A following exploration light; it does not count as crop lighting.
- **Soul Wand / Grave Tome:** Necromancy equipment requiring the corresponding research.

Custom spells can add other combinations of the supported effects. Review the current item's mana cost and cooldown in the game.
