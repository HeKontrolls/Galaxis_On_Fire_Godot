# Combat and Weapons

Combat offers melee weapons, ranged weapons, and magical equipment. Put a weapon in your hotbar, select it, and aim with the mouse.

## Melee

Left-click with a sword to attack. Wood, copper, and iron swords provide different damage and recovery times. Tools can also deal damage, but their primary material-working role differs from a dedicated weapon.

Dedicated melee weapons cannot mine. Switch to an appropriate tool when gathering materials. Hold left-click for repeated attacks; each weapon has its own recovery time.

| Weapon | Damage | Recovery | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood Spear | 16 | 0.65s | Early aimed thrust, longer reach |
| Copper Spear | 25 | 0.65s | Upgraded spear |
| Iron Spear | 34 | 0.65s | Strongest spear, up to 6.25 blocks of reach |
| Iron Dagger | 19 | 0.26s | Fast attacks at close range |
| Iron Warhammer | 52 | 0.95s | Heavy knockback; hits up to three nearby foes |
| Crystal Blade | 43 | 0.48s | Sweeping attack; hits up to two nearby foes |

Spears and daggers thrust toward the mouse. The warhammer and crystal blade swing through an aimed area. Walls and closed doors block melee attacks; heavy knockback cannot push enemies through solid terrain.

### Crafting the new weapons

Craft a **Wood Spear** in basic crafting with **3 Planks and 4 Sticks**. The remaining recipes use ingredients deposited in a **Workbench**:

- **Copper Spear:** 1 Wood Spear and 3 Copper Ingots.
- **Iron Spear:** 1 Copper Spear and 4 Iron Ingots.
- **Iron Dagger:** 3 Iron Ingots, 1 Stick, and 1 Alien Fiber.
- **Iron Warhammer:** 8 Iron Ingots, 4 Planks, and 2 Alien Fiber.
- **Crystal Blade:** 1 Iron Sword, 5 Crystal, and 2 Gold Ingots.

New games still start with empty inventories. Crafted weapons can be dragged or Ctrl-clicked into the hotbar.

## Bows and crossbows

| Weapon | Base damage | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood Bow | 14 | Hold left-click to draw, release to fire |
| Copper Bow | 20 | Hold to draw, release to fire |
| Iron Bow | 28 | Hold to draw, release to fire |
| Iron Crossbow | 36 | Click to fire; reload between shots |

These are base weapon values. Ammunition and bow draw affect the resulting shot. A quick release produces a weaker shot than a full draw.

## Ammunition

Stone, Copper, Iron, and Crystal Arrows are available. Improved arrows add damage, and Crystal Arrows can pierce two enemies. You must carry ammunition to shoot. Right-click a selected ranged weapon to cycle available ammunition.

Terrain, closed doors, and liquids affect projectile travel. Switching equipment or opening a blocking menu cancels a drawn bow rather than releasing an unintended shot.

## Magic

Wands, staffs, tomes, and ward books use mana and cooldowns. Magic supports direct damage, status effects, healing, protection, illumination, and necromancy. See [Magic and Skill Tree](Magic-and-Skill-Tree).

## Armor and shields

Fiber, copper, and iron armor reduce creature damage. Equip a shield and hold right-click with a melee weapon or empty hand to block toward the mouse. Guard is limited; lower the shield to recover it. See [Armor and Equipment](Armor-and-Equipment) for recipes, accessories, and equipment slots.

## Staying alive

Watch the shared HP/MP status panel. Carry healing food and lighting, particularly when exploring caves. Lava hurts the player, and some enemies attack by flight or close contact.

The robot and raised allies are companions; the current multiplayer mode is cooperative rather than a PvP mode.
