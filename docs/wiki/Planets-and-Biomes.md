# Planets and Biomes

A planet's climate sets its overall character, while regional biomes change the terrain and scenery along the surface. Forest planets have open areas as well as wooded regions; other climates have their own biome sets.

## Planet types

| Planet type | Surface biomes |
| --- | --- |
| Temperate forest and grasslands | Meadow, Woodland, Dense forest, Rocky uplands |
| Scattered islands and shallow sea | Sandy shore, Coastal grove, Island jungle, Tidal banks |
| Mixed oceans and continents | Coastal grassland, Continental forest, Rocky coast, Sandbars |
| Hot desert and dunes | Dune sea, Rocky badlands, Oasis, Stony desert |
| Rocky inner world and lava plains | Basalt plains, Lava fields, Obsidian flats, Volcanic ridges |
| Outer rocky barren / cratered | Crater basins, Rocky highlands, Dust plains, Shattered ridges |
| Frozen ocean and ice floes | Pack ice, Fractured ice, Glacial shelf, Open leads |
| Tundra and permafrost | Snowfields, Boreal woodland, Exposed rock, Glacial valleys |

## Planet danger

The solar-system viewer shows a stable danger rating for each climate. It affects hostile surface, cave, and newly arriving night creatures; the quest guardian has its own fixed stats.

| Danger | Planets | Enemy HP | Enemy damage | Normal material drops per kill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 - Frontier | Temperate forest; scattered islands | Base | Base | 1 |
| 2 - Hazardous | Mixed oceans; hot desert; tundra | +25% | +20% | 2 |
| 3 - Extreme | Volcanic inner; outer rocky barren; frozen ocean | +50% | +40% | 3 |

Health and damage values round to whole numbers. Existing injured creatures keep their health percentage when upgrading an older save; revisiting a world does not stack these bonuses. The material type stays the same, and the multiplier does not increase special quest rewards or Soul-study Bone Fragments.

Defeat the [Mining Guardian](Mining-Guardian), improve your armor, and use the optional ship survey array to choose your next destination. Travel to higher-danger worlds is not locked behind the survey upgrade.

## Climate affects exploration

Terrain materials, relief, tree density, flora, wildlife, and liquids vary with climate and biome. Biome backgrounds provide additional depth and scenery as you travel.

Temperate forest planets belong in the star's habitable zone and provide the starting environment for normal expedition progression. Deserts offer sparse vegetation and oases. Frozen oceans feature ice formations and towering spires. Volcanic inner worlds contain lava landscapes and ash or ember weather instead of ordinary water rain.

## Underground biomes

Caves have a separate set of environments, ranging from root caverns and aquifers to crystal, fungal, basalt, and molten regions. Surface climate does not describe every underground location.

See [Underground Exploration](Underground-Exploration) for cave preparation and [Farming and Food](Farming-and-Food) for climate-sensitive growing conditions.
