# Building and Housing

Build a foothold with shelter, storage, work areas, lighting, and routes through difficult terrain.

## Placing items

Assign a placeable item to the hotbar, select it, and right-click a valid location within reach. Terrain blocks, stations, and furniture have different space and support requirements.

Workbench, furnace, and chest items are placeable world objects. Aim at a placed object and press **F** to use it.

## Furniture and building pieces

| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Wooden Chair | Interior furnishing |
| Wooden Table | Interior furnishing |
| Wooden Ladder | Vertical travel using W/S or Up/Down; Space jumps off |
| Wooden Door | F opens or closes it; a closed door blocks passage |
| Wooden Wall | Background wood for buildings |
| Fireplace | F lights or extinguishes it; provides light when lit |
| Chest | Shared world storage accessed with F |
| Torch | Local lighting for shelters and caves |

Background walls are distinct from solid foreground blocks. Use the correct building piece for the appearance and passage you want. Hammer/mattock interactions support background work.

## Choosing a site

Leave room around doors, ladders, and station footprints. Keep an accessible path to R0-VR and the escape pod. Inspect nearby slopes and water before excavating a basement: opening a connected basin or aquifer can bring liquid into the new space.

Natural trees and vegetation are passable scenery. They do not act like placed solid wood blocks.

## A useful first shelter

Provide a roof, lighting, a workbench, a furnace, and storage. Put a small farm nearby, or create a covered growing area with suitable light. A lit shelter and a first corn harvest are part of the early expedition objectives.

Fireplaces can be extinguished by water. Powered lamps provide another lighting option once your electrical network is ready.

Buildings and station inventories belong to the saved world. In multiplayer, other captains can use shared storage and work areas.
