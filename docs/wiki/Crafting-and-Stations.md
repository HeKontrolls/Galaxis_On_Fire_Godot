# Crafting and Stations

Crafting turns gathered materials into equipment, structures, food, electrical components, and magical supplies.

## Basic crafting

Press **C** for basic crafting or **M** for the build/crafting menu. Select a recipe and review its listed ingredients. Basic recipes use your carried materials.

Useful starting recipes include:

| Result | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| 4 Planks | 1 Wood |
| 4 Sticks | 1 Plank |
| Workbench | 8 Planks |
| Chest | 8 Planks, 2 Sticks |
| 4 Torches | 1 Stick, 1 Coal Ore |

The recipe list in the current build is the reference for complete costs and station requirements.

## Workbench

1. Put a workbench in your hotbar and place it on suitable ground.
2. Aim at it and press **F**.
3. Move ingredients into the workbench's input slots.
4. Choose a recipe and craft it, using its button or **Enter**.
5. Take the completed items from the output slots.

Workbench materials are stored in that instance. A second bench has its own inventory. Ingredients in your backpack or another bench do not automatically satisfy its recipe.

## Furnace

Place a furnace and open it with **F**. Put ores or cooking ingredients into its input slots and the recipe's fuel into the fuel slot. Valid recipes are fed into the processing queue automatically.

A cold furnace takes approximately **five seconds** to heat before processing. Individual recipes then take additional time: copper ingots take 8 seconds, iron 11 seconds, and gold 9 seconds. Take finished goods from the output storage.

Heat, queued work, ingredients, and completed output are included in saves. Crafting is not an instant conversion of every ore in your backpack.

## Other stations

Generators have fuel storage. Cauldrons brew reagents, and alchemy desks inscribe custom magical equipment. Open their individual windows with **F**.

Drag station windows by their title bars and close them using their close buttons or the applicable interaction/escape control.
