# Inventory and Hotbar

The backpack stores your possessions. The hotbar provides shortcuts to the tools, blocks, food, and other items you want to use quickly.

## Backpack

Press **I** or click the backpack shortcut to open it. The backpack displays items in pages of slots. Use its sort button to organize the displayed inventory.

Item quantities belong to the inventory. Assigning an item to the hotbar does not create another stack or grant additional items.

## Hotbar

There are **six pages of nine slots**, for 54 shortcuts.

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Select a slot on the current page | 1–9 |
| Cycle slots on the current page | Mouse wheel or Tab |
| Change pages with the wheel | Hold Alt while scrolling |
| Cycle pages | B |
| Add an inventory item to a free slot | Ctrl + left-click the item |
| Assign or move a shortcut | Drag to a hotbar slot |
| Remove a shortcut | Ctrl + click its hotbar slot |

Tools must be owned and assigned/equipped to use them. Selecting an empty slot provides an empty hand. Removing a shortcut does not destroy the inventory item.

## Trash

The backpack has a trash area. Move an unwanted item into it, then press the **red X** to destroy the staged item. Staging and destroying are separate actions, allowing you to review what is being discarded.

## Stations and storage

Chests, workbenches, furnaces, and alchemy stations have their own storage. Open them with **F** and move items between their slots and your backpack. Recipes that use a station's inventory require materials in that station.

Players in co-op have separate backpacks but can access shared world storage and stations.

## Equipment

Click or right-click armor, shields, and accessories in your backpack to equip them. Use the **Gear** tab to inspect equipment slots and return worn items to your backpack. See [Armor and Equipment](Armor-and-Equipment).
