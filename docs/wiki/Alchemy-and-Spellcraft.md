# Alchemy and Spellcraft

Alchemy provides magical reagents, and spellcraft turns unlocked effects into custom wands, staffs, and tomes. Designs use the game's supported effects and delivery forms, with progression through the skill tree.

## Stations

Place a **Cauldron** or **Alchemy Desk** on supported ground, aim at it, and press **F**. Each station has its own reagent and output storage. Put ingredients into that station rather than leaving them only in the backpack.

## Brewing

The cauldron produces Fire, Frost, Storm, Life, and Spirit Essences, along with Arcane Ink and Mana Tonics. Choose a recipe, supply its ingredients, and start the brew. Brewing takes time; collect the finished output afterward.

Recipes use resources such as coal, crystal, glacial ice, copper, produce, and alien fiber. Check the station's ingredient list for the exact recipe in your build.

## Inscribe a spell

1. Open **K** and learn the effect and delivery skills you want.
2. Place a blank Wand, Staff, or Tome and the required Arcane Ink and essences into an alchemy desk.
3. Choose the matching focus, a delivery form, and a primary effect.
4. Add a second different effect if **Dual weaving** is unlocked.
5. Adjust the supported strength, duration, and area settings.
6. Name the design and inscribe it.
7. Collect the new item and assign it to your hotbar.

Raise Dead inscriptions also require Bone Fragments. The station checks ingredients, unlocks, and design validity before consuming materials.

## Effects and forms

Supported effects include Ember, Frost, Storm, Crystal, Mending, Regeneration, Ward, Light, Drain, Blight, and Raise Dead.

| Form | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Bolt | Directed projectile delivery |
| Self | Support effects centered on the caster |
| Burst | Area delivery after its research unlock |
| Chain | Linked targeting after its research unlock |

Support-only designs use Self delivery. Stronger settings require sufficient mastery and increase costs where applicable. Duration only changes effects that use duration; area tuning applies to Burst and Chain.

## Your spell library

Inscribed designs belong to the character's spell library and receive unique item names. That library is included in saves. The current library supports up to 64 designs; station output must also have room.
