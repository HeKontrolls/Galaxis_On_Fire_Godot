# Armor and Equipment

Equip armor, a shield, and accessories to prepare for nighttime encounters and cave exploration. All equipment is crafted at a **Workbench** using ingredients placed in its input slots.

## Equipping and removing items

Open your backpack with **I**. Click or right-click an equipment item to wear it. The **Gear** button switches to six equipment slots: helmet, chest armor, boots, shield, and two accessories. Click an occupied slot to return its item to the backpack.

Equipping transfers one physical item out of your backpack. Replacing it returns the previous item. Equipped items cannot be consumed by crafting or destroyed through backpack trash. To upgrade an equipped shield, first return it to your backpack and deposit it in the workbench.

Ctrl-click still adds a backpack item to the hotbar. Selecting and using an equipment shortcut equips that item. You can also drag an owned item onto a matching slot while the Gear tab is open. New characters start without free armor.

## Armor progression

| Set | Helmet defense | Chest defense | Boots defense | Total defense | Movement penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fiber | 1 | 2 | 1 | 4 | None |
| Copper | 2 | 4 | 2 | 8 | 5% |
| Iron | 3 | 6 | 3 | 12 | 10% |

Mix pieces freely. Each two defense points remove one point of incoming creature damage, rounded down. Armor alone cannot reduce a damaging hit below one. Lava remains hazardous and bypasses this physical protection.

Armor appears on both character models and follows idle, walking, and jumping animations. Removing a helmet restores your chosen hairstyle.

## Shields and guard

With a shield equipped, select a melee weapon or an empty hotbar slot and **hold right-click**. Aim the mouse toward incoming attacks. Shields cover a 120-degree arc around your aim; attacks from behind remain dangerous. Blocking slows movement and prevents melee attacks. Finish your attack recovery before raising the shield.

| Shield | Damage blocked after armor | Workbench ingredients |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood Shield | 65% | 6 Planks + 2 Alien Fiber |
| Copper Shield | 75% | 1 Wood Shield + 4 Copper Ingots |
| Iron Shield | 85% | 1 Copper Shield + 6 Iron Ingots |

Successful blocks spend guard. If a hit costs more guard than you have, your guard breaks and the shield does not reduce that hit. Release right-click to recover guard after a short delay. Watch the guard meter beneath the health panel. Shields do not replace magic wards: armor applies first, then the shield, then any active ward absorbs the remaining damage.

Tools, building items, bows, and magic retain their existing right-click actions. Menus, focus loss, and switching equipment lower the shield.

## Accessories

| Accessory | Effect | Workbench ingredients |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout Charm | +8% movement speed | 8 Alien Fiber + 1 Crystal |
| Bone Ward | +2 defense | 6 Bone Fragments + 4 Alien Fiber |

Equip up to two accessories, with only one of each type. Bone Fragments currently drop after learning **Soul study** in the necromancy research branch. Alien Fiber from defeated creatures supplies early armor and shield recipes.

Equipment and guard recovery are saved with your character. In multiplayer, each captain has separate equipment, and armor and raised shields are visible to other players.
